# Bug Bounty Program

The bounty engine tracks submissions, reviewer approvals, severity rewards, payout accounting, duplicate fingerprints, and responsible disclosure embargoes.

## Reward Tiers

| Severity | Reward |
|---|---:|
| Critical | 5000 |
| High | 2000 |
| Medium | 500 |
| Low | 100 |

Rewards are paid from the bounty pool after the configured approval threshold is met. High-value payouts can require a timelock before release.

## Submission Rules

- Include a concise impact statement, reproduction steps, affected component, and suggested remediation.
- Submissions are fingerprinted so duplicate reports can be rejected deterministically.
- Public disclosure is embargoed for 90 days unless maintainers approve earlier disclosure.
- Accepted reports are eligible for hall-of-fame attribution when the reporter opts in.

## Governance Budget

The bounty budget should be funded by DAO-approved treasury allocations. Program changes, reward tier changes, or emergency pauses should be proposed through governance.
